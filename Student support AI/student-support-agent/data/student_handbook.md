# Student Policy Handbook (PGDM Batch 2025-27)

## [POL-FEE-01] Fee payment schedule
Term fees are payable in full before the term due date shown in the student's fee record. Payments can be made through the online payment portal, by demand draft, or at the accounts office counter. A receipt is generated automatically after every successful payment and can be downloaded from the student portal under Fees > Receipts.

## [POL-FEE-02] Late payment fee
If the due amount is not cleared by the due date, a late fee of 1% of the outstanding amount per week (or part of a week) is charged, up to a maximum of 5% of the term fee. Students with an overdue balance beyond 30 days may be barred from term-end examinations until the dues are cleared.

## [POL-FEE-03] Payment portal failure or downtime
If the payment portal fails, times out, or the amount is debited but the payment is not confirmed, the student must raise a support request with the transaction reference number and a screenshot within 24 hours. If the accounts office verifies the failure, the student may complete the payment on the next working day without any late fee. Payments made on the next working day without a verified support request will attract the normal late fee. A debited amount that was not credited is refunded automatically within 7 working days.

## [POL-FEE-04] Instalment requests
Students who cannot pay the full term fee at once may request a two-instalment plan from the accounts office before the due date. The second instalment must be paid within 30 days of the original due date. Instalment plans are approved by the accounts office, not by faculty or programme chairs.

## [POL-FEE-05] Fee refunds
Fees are refundable only if the student withdraws from the programme. A student who withdraws within 15 days of term start receives an 80% refund of the term fee, between 16 and 30 days a 50% refund, and no refund after 30 days. Refunds are processed within 21 working days to the original payment method.

## [POL-SCH-01] Scholarship types and eligibility
The institute offers Merit Scholarships (for students with a CGPA of 3.5 or above in the previous term), Need-Based Aid (for families under the stated income limit, with income documents) and special grants announced during the year. A student can hold only one institute scholarship per term.

## [POL-SCH-02] Scholarship renewal and credit
Merit Scholarships are renewed every term if the student keeps a CGPA of at least 3.0. Approved scholarship amounts are adjusted against the next term fee, not paid out in cash. The scholarship committee meets once a month and the decision is shown in the student's scholarship status.

## [POL-ACA-01] Course and elective change
A student may request a change of elective course within the first 14 days of the term, subject to seat availability and approval by the programme chair. A change of programme (for example between Big Data Analytics and AI & Data Science) is allowed only once and only before the end of Term 1, with approval from the registrar.

## [POL-ACA-02] Attendance requirement
A minimum of 75% attendance is required in every course to be eligible for the end-term examination. Students with medical leave must submit documents to the programme office within 7 days of returning to class.

## [POL-ACA-03] Exam re-evaluation
A student may apply for re-evaluation of an end-term answer script within 7 days of result declaration by paying the re-evaluation fee at the accounts office. The result of re-evaluation is final.

## [POL-REC-01] Changing personal details on the student record
Changes to the registered email address, phone number or programme on a student's record are sensitive changes. They must be requested by the student and are applied only after approval by a registrar office staff member. The student is informed once the change is approved or rejected.

## [POL-REC-02] Privacy and credentials
Students can only view and request changes to their own records. Staff and the support assistant never ask for passwords, OTPs, CVV numbers or card numbers. Students must not share these in chat, email or support requests. Any such information that is shared by mistake is not stored.

## [POL-SUP-01] Support requests
A support request can be raised for fee, scholarship, academic or technical problems. The accounts office responds to fee-related requests within 2 working days and other requests within 3 working days. Every request gets a reference number starting with SR.
